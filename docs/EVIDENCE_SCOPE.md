# Evidence scope

The repository separates four evidence layers that should not be conflated.

## A. Exact design

The finite synthetic support and posterior formulas define which audited public statistic determines the target posterior. IS0--IS2 are the pure-relocation conditions: within each opportunity level, they share the same two-point posterior marginal. IS3 is an interaction extension with matched Bayes Brier opportunity but a different posterior shape.

These are exact properties of the frozen synthetic design.

## B. Development learner results

Stage V contains 2,304 frozen-protocol learner runs. The primary estimands are six within-condition paired size-integrated normalized-regret contrasts.

The six corpus values are the independent observations for each primary contrast. These are development estimates, not confirmatory family-level superiority results and not a causal learner-by-regime interaction.

IS0 is an implementation-gap control. Its Tabular-minus-Static gaps constrain interpretation of structural-condition rankings but do not identify the cause of those gaps.

## C. Selected diagnostics

The diagnostic artifacts concern selected trajectories chosen after official outcomes were inspected. They show that, in those selected cases, a learned content pathway can lose a prediction-relevant distinction while the public input information remains available.

They do not estimate how often this happens and do not establish that this mechanism explains the official rankings as a whole.

## D. Selected interventions

The learning-rate continuations start from exact selected prestates.

For selected D1, a persistent reduction to 0.0001 prevented complete content-path inactivity and ended at 97.08273892812468% normalized Brier recovery. A one-step reduction followed by restoration to 0.001 did not sustain the pathway. For selected D3, the same nominal persistent reduction did not prevent immediate pathway inactivity.

These are local interventions, not a general learning-rate recommendation and not evidence that one model family is intrinsically more recoverable.

## Confirmatory stage

Stage C was not executed. The allocation calculation selected the cap of 32 corpora but the worst planned half-width remained about 0.1553, above the predeclared 0.10 precision target. No confirmatory result should be inferred from the development evidence.
